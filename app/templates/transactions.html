Issued to cardholders. Decreases Shop balance. Rejects over-distribution if quantity exceeds Shop stock.

Record Stock Adjustment (Reason Required)

Moisture variation or inventory reconciliation. Deductions cannot result in negative stock. Reason strictly mandatory.

Official inspection entry. Automatically compared against derived stock. Discrepancies generate neutral verification alerts.
